# AXIOM is an ecosystem, not a single project

AXIOM is the umbrella for a set of related engineering efforts. The projects below have distinct roles and boundaries; together they explore how an extensible AI system can plan, validate, execute, retain knowledge and connect capabilities.

## AXIOM — the exosystem

The overarching architecture and integration vision: a capability-oriented environment with explicit subsystem responsibilities, replaceable intelligence providers, controlled execution and persistent context.

**Focus:** architecture, contracts, capability boundaries and system integration.

## AXIOM Forge

The validation and controlled-evolution track. Its purpose is to make changes verifiable and upgrades safer through explicit checks, contracts and evidence.

**Focus:** verification, validation and safe evolution.

## AXIOM Wild

The adapter and integration track for connecting external tools, providers and capabilities to the AXIOM environment without collapsing their boundaries.

**Focus:** adapters, provider integration and execution interfaces.

## AXIOM Big Planner

A planning system for turning goals into structured missions and deterministic workflows. Planning is treated as a bounded engineering component, not as an unrestricted model-driven execution loop.

**Focus:** mission planning, workflow construction and epistemic boundaries.

## AXIOM Knowledge

A persistent knowledge space intended to capture, organize, index and compact useful information over time.

**Focus:** continuity, retrieval, indexing and knowledge maintenance.

## AXIOM Lab

An experimentation environment for evaluating models, integrations and planning approaches with reproducible tests and recorded results.

**Focus:** experiments, model evaluation and engineering evidence.

## AXIOM CRE

A continuous research and opportunity-exploration capability intended to monitor information, identify signals and help redirect attention as evidence changes.

**Focus:** research workflows, opportunity discovery and decision support.

## Infrastructure resilience

Practical work involving virtualization, network segmentation, backup verification, storage, disaster recovery and dependable operations.

**Focus:** operational reliability and recoverability.

## Cloud architecture

A developing portfolio of AWS and Terraform exercises focused on architecture fundamentals, repeatable infrastructure and operational trade-offs.

**Focus:** cloud design, infrastructure as code and hands-on learning.
