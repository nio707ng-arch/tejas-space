I am an engineering and infrastructure professional with hands-on experience across Linux and Windows environments, networking, virtualization, storage, backup systems and business-critical infrastructure.

My work is grounded in operating real systems: diagnosing failures, improving reliability, planning migrations and making infrastructure easier to maintain. I am extending that foundation into cloud architecture, infrastructure as code and AI systems engineering.

## Engineering interests

- **Infrastructure and operations:** Linux, Windows Server, virtualization, networking, storage, backup and recovery.
- **Cloud and automation:** AWS, Terraform, repeatable deployment and operational discipline.
- **AI systems:** modular capability architectures, deterministic planning, knowledge continuity, integration boundaries and validation.

## How I work

I prefer evidence over assumptions. I break complex systems into explicit responsibilities, test the interfaces and failure paths, document decisions, and improve designs incrementally.

This site is a working portfolio. Project descriptions distinguish active engineering work from experiments and learning tracks.
