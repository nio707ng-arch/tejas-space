A technical portfolio becomes more useful when it records not only outcomes, but also the reasoning behind them.

For each experiment, capture:

1. The problem and constraints.
2. The architecture or approach selected.
3. The implementation steps.
4. What failed and how it was diagnosed.
5. The result, evidence and remaining limitations.
